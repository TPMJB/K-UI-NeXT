K-UI G1 Bridge Rev A — native circuit implementation, 10 October 2026

Open KUI-G1-Bridge-RevA.kicad_pro with KiCad 10. The root schematic links
eleven physical circuit sheets. U10 is the MachXO2-2000 front end; U201 is
RP2350B; U203 is 8 MiB PSRAM. J50/J51 receive independent CN503 flex arms.

Power enters J20 as a separate 5 V/GND pair. The flex's 5 V contacts are
isolated from that supply. U206 represents the manual USB-free C5 module;
K201–K216 are actual Harwin spring contacts. A 0.90 mm supported removable
clamp is required. C5 belongs in the 8 mm clearance region.

The native schematic passes ERC with zero violations. Physical electrical
pad numbers are checked against every loaded footprint. Both passive flex
arms are fully routed and pass ERC/DRC/parity. The carrier PCB is a layout
draft: read its current check report before judging fabrication readiness.

All optional BIOS circuitry is drawn and DNP for BASE; stock recovery is
a physical selector path in BIOS_OPTION. The reserve capacitor bank is DNP.
Neither provision establishes automatic bank switching or write retention.
BASE_PSRAM_DNP omits only U203 and requires firmware to leave CS1 inactive.

Source contracts: ../design/blocks/*.json. Regenerate circuit/footprints with
/usr/bin/python3 ../tools/generate_design.py after installing KiCad 10.
The FPGA logic, simulation checks and byte-link contract are in ../fpga/.
Complete RP2350 ATA/media firmware and a placed/routed timed FPGA image are
not supplied yet. Firmware must not advertise DMA during initial bring-up.
Bridge output activation also requires the documented two-frame vendor unlock;
hardware ARM alone is insufficient. Raw G1 probe mappings are in
../design/raw-g1-debug.md.

The reference/ directory preserves the original MCU-only starter. It is
historical and its direct-ATA GPIO allocation does not apply to this circuit.
Manufacturing status and the current parts list are in ../manufacturing/.
